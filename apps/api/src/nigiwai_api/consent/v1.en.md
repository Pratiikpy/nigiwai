You are about to pick a few things you love. Here is exactly what happens.

**What we collect.** The things you pick (artists, shows, films, books, brands, podcasts, games), which poster you scanned, the day and hour you answer, and, only if you choose to add them, an age range, the area you live in and a budget level.

**What we never collect.** Your name, email, phone number, device ID or location. There is no account, and nothing is saved on your phone except to finish this survey.

**Who sees it.** The venue that put up this poster sees groups of 10 or more people who picked similar things. They never see your answers on their own.

**Qloo.** To find what you type we use Qloo, a taste data service. The words you type in the search box and the item you choose are sent to Qloo. Nothing about you is, and none of it is linked to your answers. Qloo may keep and reuse them under its own terms.

**Your choices.** Answer only what you want. When you finish you get a receipt code. Enter it on this survey page, under "Remove my answers", to delete your answers at any time.

This survey is for people 18 and over.
