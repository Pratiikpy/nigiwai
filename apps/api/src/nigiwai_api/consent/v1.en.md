You are about to pick a few things you love. Here is exactly what happens.

**What we collect.** The things you pick (artists, shows, films, brands, podcasts, games), the day and hour you answer, and, only if you choose to add them, an age range, the area you live in and a budget level.

**What we never collect.** Your name, email, phone number, device ID or location. There is no account, and nothing is saved on your phone except to finish this survey.

**Who sees it.** The venue that put up this poster sees groups of 10 or more people who picked similar things. They never see your answers on their own.

**Qloo.** To look up what you pick we use Qloo, a taste data service. Qloo may keep and reuse anonymous picks like these under its own terms. We send Qloo only the names you pick and never anything about you.

**Your choices.** Answer only what you want. When you finish you get a receipt code; enter it on the same page to remove your answers.

This survey is for people 18 and over.
